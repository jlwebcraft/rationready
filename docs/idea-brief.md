# RationReady — Idea Brief

> **Product line:** Know before you go.  
> **Competition:** Techfest 2026–27 — Campus to Cloud Hackathon sponsored by AWS  
> **Status:** Draft / Foundation Phase

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Beneficiaries travel to Fair Price Shops (ration shops) without knowing whether the shop is crowded, whether the biometric electronic Point of Sale (ePOS) device is functioning, or whether listed commodities are currently available. A wasted visit costs daily-wage earners time, money, and physical effort.

## 2. Solution: RationReady

A community-powered, time-aware PDS visit assistant that provides crowdsourced, freshness-labeled operational updates:

1. Queue length and waiting time (2-hour expiry window).
2. ePOS terminal status (4-hour majority consensus).
3. Commodity availability (24-hour validity).

## 3. Pilot Boundary

- One demonstration locality: `Demo Locality A`.
- 5 synthetic demonstration shops visibly marked `DEMO DATA`.
- No sensitive personal data, Aadhaar, or ration-card numbers collected.
